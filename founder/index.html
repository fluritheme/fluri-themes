František Gejdoš, also written as Frantisek Gejdos, is the founder of Fluri Themes and the creator of its Winter, Spider, Grinch, Black Cat, Ghost, Rainy Window and Tiny Builders website effects.

Fluri Themes is an independent collection of interactive TypeScript packages that add interactive seasonal details to websites.

THE PROJECTS

Winter ↗ adds snowfall that settles on real page surfaces, with snowballs, pointer interactions and optional Christmas effects. The npm packages are @fluritheme/fluri and @fluritheme/fluri-react.

Spider ↗ adds a curious jumping spider that roams the page and weaves persistent silk around content. The npm package is @fluritheme/spider.

Grinch ↗ introduces a mischievous holiday visitor with gift heists, hiding and a change of heart. Try the live playground and explore the commercial licence for production use.

Black Cat ↗ brings curious eyes, real perches, yarn play and little naps to a website. It is included in the Fluri lifetime licence.

Ghost ↗ is a shy little visitor with fluid floating, hiding, peeking and a very questionable disguise.

Rainy Window ↗ adds merging glass droplets, wandering streams and a window visitors can wipe.

Tiny Builders ↗ turns real page edges into work sites for a coordinated miniature construction crew.
